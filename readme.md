### Abstract

The focus of this research is to discover the impact of L2 regularization strength (λ) as the network’s width increases – for a fixed-layer neural network. Data involved in the research is primary and collected using machine learning experiments. Results showed the relationship between maximum λ and network width to have an exponentially negative relationship. With a correlation coefficient of \-0.982, the relationship is concluded to be very strong. In addition, the measurements of spread of the networks’ evaluated losses and accuracies reveal that smaller networks are more stable than larger ones – inferred through the trend of increase in interquartile ranges and standard deviation with network width. Furthermore, a coefficient of determination of 0.836 is found for the relationship between network width and the positive impact of L2 at reducing its magnitude of overfit – concluding that approximately 84% of the effectiveness in overfit reduction can be attributed to an increase in network width.
